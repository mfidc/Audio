Every entry carries one of these tags:
- **[V]** confirmed this session on a fetched primary page (author by-line, repo page, star count).

## Key question 1: Who are the most cited or referenced people in agent-building discussions in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
The centre of gravity is a small set of lab staff who publish how their own harnesses work (Anthropic's Applied AI / Claude Code / Platform teams, OpenAI's Codex team), plus the builders of a handful of very widely adopted open-source agent tools and skill packs; by November 2026 "harness engineering" has joined "context engineering" as the organising term, and the most-starred artefacts are skill collections and personal agents rather than frameworks. Conference line-ups for 2026 (AI Engineer World's Fair, Code w/ Claude, DevDay) could not be checked, so 2026 keynote attribution is a gap.

## Key question 3: Who are the credible sceptics and critics whose objections practitioners take seriously?

### Takeaway
The sceptics practitioners engage with fall into four camps: evaluation scientists who show agent claims are over-stated (Narayanan/Kapoor, METR, Remote Labor Index authors), security researchers who show agents with tools are structurally exploitable (Willison, Rehberger, Tramèr, Schneier, Whittaker), working engineers who report that the productivity arithmetic does not add up for them, and capability sceptics who argue LLM agents lack planning or continual learning. Labs themselves now publish sceptic-compatible material (postmortems, noise in evals, false-negative rates).

### Inferences
- The memory debate has shifted from "vector store vs knowledge graph" (Mem0, Zep, 2024-2025) to "agent-maintained files and wikis with offline consolidation" (Karpathy gist, Letta sleep-time compute, Anthropic dreaming, hermes-agent), which favours voices from the personal-knowledge-management world (Ango, Forte, Torres, Brier).
- Non-coding guidance from credible sources converges on the same few practices as coding guidance: state the goal and stop conditions, give the agent your files, ask for the finished artefact, and review.

### Gaps
- Non-coding voices are the weakest-verified part of this roster: only Todorova, Sant'Anna, Luu and the Harvey benchmark were confirmed this session.
- Could not check whether Tiago Forte, Teresa Torres, Noah Brier or Steph Ango published major agent-memory pieces after mid-2026.
- No verified source on domain-specific agent voices in law, medicine, finance or science beyond the names given from memory.
